# Changelog

## 1.0

The first release.

### Timer

- Every WCA event and FTO, with scrambles in their official written form.
- Start and stop with the space bar, two hands on the keyboard, or a touch. Times can also be typed in.
- Inspection as at a competition: 15 seconds, calls at 8 and 12, +2 and DNF given automatically when you run over. It can be switched off.
- +2, DNF, a comment and delete on any solve, with undo. Deleted solves and sessions wait in the trash for 30 days.
- A picture of the scrambled puzzle. A click enlarges it.
- Earlier scrambles stay within reach, and a past scramble can be solved again.
- The cross solution for a 3×3 scramble can be shown beside it.
- Fewest Moves solutions are checked against the scramble. Multi-Blind is entered as cubes and time.
- While solving, the time can run in hundredths, tenths or whole seconds, or stay hidden. The screen stays on while you practise.
- Every action has a key, the keys can be changed, and a command palette finds the rest.

### Sessions and statistics

- Sessions for each event: new, rename, delete, archive, reorder by drag, and group into folders.
- Merge two sessions of one puzzle, split a session at a solve, move solves between sessions.
- Averages of any size, mo3, best and mean, computed and rounded as the WCA does. An average opens to show its solves and can be copied as text.
- An average that is still short of solves says how many are left.
- Progress: the trend with personal bests marked, how the average has moved, the distribution of times, and a calendar of practice days.
- Stays quick with a very large history.

### Trainer

- Algorithms for the 3×3: F2L, last slot, OLL, PLL, COLL and CMLL. For the 2×2: OLL, PBL and CLL.
- Each case has a picture, an algorithm split into triggers, and a short note on how to recognise it.
- Learn shows the cases, Drill gives a scramble that leads to a chosen case and times it, Review brings back what is due and what went slowly.
- Drill scrambles in the timer: a cross or xcross in a set number of turns, cross solved, chosen last-layer cases, edges only, corners only, R and U only.
- Cross colour training: a colour is assigned to each scramble and kept with the solve, and Progress shows your times by colour.
- The colours you hold on top and in front can be set, and the pictures and scrambles follow them.

### Import and backups

- Import your history from csTimer, CubeTime, Twisty Timer and CubeDesk. A preview comes first, and importing the same file twice adds nothing.
- Export as a cubehaus file, in csTimer's format, or as CSV.
- All data can be saved as one file and restored from it.
- The desktop app keeps the history in a file on disk and writes backups to a folder you choose.
- The iPhone app keeps a copy of the history in its Documents folder, where the phone's own backup includes it.

### Apps

- Web: works in a current browser on a computer or a phone, and offline after the first visit. A new version is picked up when no solve is in progress.
- macOS: an app for Apple silicon and Intel, signed and notarized, that updates itself. To find an update it asks GitHub for the latest release and sends nothing else.
- iPhone: the same timer as an app, with haptics and the share sheet for files.
- No account. Your solves stay on your device.
- Dark and light themes, your own theme with a background picture, and three layouts for the timer screen.
- English and Russian.
